The Beginning

Why did Rizal write the novel?

The creation of Noli Me Tangere was influenced by Rizal's desire to portray the suffering and oppression experienced by Filipinos under Spanish colonial rule.

An important inspiration

Uncle Tom's Cabin

Harriet Beecher Stowe

A story that moved Rizal

Uncle Tom's Cabin, written by Harriet Beecher Stowe, dealt with the trials and hardships experienced by enslaved Black people.

The suffering presented in the novel awakened Rizal's sympathy for people who were oppressed and afflicted.

Rizal was inspired to use literature to portray the suffering experienced by Filipinos under Spanish colonial rule.

The Struggle to Print

The novel was written. But could it be printed?

Finishing the manuscript did not mean the journey was over. Rizal still needed money to have the novel printed.

01

Fernando Canon

In December 1886, Rizal was worried about the novel because he could not afford its printing. He shared his problem with Fernando Canon and even mentioned that he had almost thrown the manuscript into the fire.

02

Maximo Viola

Maximo Viola helped Rizal by lending him money so that the novel could finally be printed.

The Berlin Incident

Was Rizal a spy?

While in Berlin, Rizal encountered a misunderstanding that placed him under suspicion. His movements around rural villages led authorities to suspect that he might be a French spy.

Suspicion

The Chief of Police received an intelligence report that Rizal was regularly visiting rural villages and was suspected of acting as a French spy.

The Passport Problem

Rizal was asked to show his passport, but he did not have one. He became concerned that he could be deported.

The Four-Day Period

Rizal attempted to obtain a passport from the Spanish embassy, but they failed to issue one. He was given four days to present himself to the Chief of Police.

Rizal Explains

Rizal explained in fluent German that he was a Filipino doctor whose interest was local culture and ethnology, not espionage.

His honesty and sincerity persuaded the Chief of Police to change his mind and allow him to remain in Germany.

The Title

José Rizal explained that the title Noli Me Tangere means “Touch Me Not.” He derived this phrase from the Bible, specifically from the scene on the first Easter Sunday when Mary Magdalene visited the Holy Sepulcher. To her, our Lord Jesus said:

Touch me not; I am not yet ascended to my Father, but go to my brethren, and say unto them, I ascend unto my Father; and to my God and your God.” (John 20:13–17)
PH
Rizal dedicated the novel to the Philippines, his fatherland. He stated that the facts presented in the novel were true and had happened, and that he could prove them.
